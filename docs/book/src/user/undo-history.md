# Undo History

The **Undo History** panel lists scene edits from the opened or saved scene through the current step. New steps appear at the bottom of the list. The row in bold is where the scene is now. Steps waiting on the redo stack appear grey and italic below the current step.

Open the panel from **Edit > Undo History** or **Window > Undo History**. The footer shows how many steps are stored, the configured step limit, and an estimated memory use for the stack. Click a row to jump the scene to that point in one step.

The top row is the **Opened Scene** state from when the project was loaded or last saved successfully. Closing and reopening the panel does not clear the list. The panel scroll position is saved with the editor session.
